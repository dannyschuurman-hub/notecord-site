About NoteCord and your privacy

NoteCord is made by Danny Schuurman, an independent maker in the Netherlands. The app records, transcribes and summarizes conversations on your iPhone or iPad. Recordings and text stay on your device. We cannot see them.

Download NoteCord

You want to know who makes the app.

Before recording a conversation, you want to know who is behind the app and where the recording goes. The App Store lists Danny Schuurman as NoteCord’s seller. The app processes your conversations on your own device.

Made by Danny Schuurman in the Netherlands

Danny Schuurman is the independent maker of NoteCord. His name also appears as the seller in the App Store. For questions about the app, email danny@apexpartnerships.nl.

Your recordings never reach us.

NoteCord records, transcribes and summarizes on your device. You use the app without an account. There is no NoteCord server. Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. Audio is excluded from every backup. Text is included only in your iPhone’s standard iCloud backup if enabled, in your own iCloud.

Check it yourself.

Visit notecord.nl/check-notecord-privacy-yourself to read what we measured and what the test covers. The measurement took place on November 12, 2026 in the iPhone simulator on a Mac. The page also explains how to view network activity on your own iPhone using App Privacy Report. You can check the privacy label in the App Store too.

Questions people ask

Who makes NoteCord?
NoteCord is made by Danny Schuurman, an independent maker in the Netherlands. The App Store lists Danny Schuurman as the seller.
Where is NoteCord based?
NoteCord’s maker, Danny Schuurman, is based in the Netherlands. You can reach him at danny@apexpartnerships.nl.
How do I get in touch?
Email danny@apexpartnerships.nl. You can also find NoteCord on Instagram as @notecordapp.
Does NoteCord sell my data?
No. NoteCord does not collect your data. Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. We cannot see your recordings or text. There is no NoteCord server.
How can I check NoteCord’s privacy promise?
Read “Check it yourself” at notecord.nl/check-notecord-privacy-yourself. It includes the measurement, the test’s limits and steps to view network activity on your iPhone. Check NoteCord’s privacy label in the App Store too.
Are my recordings or texts included in a backup?
Audio is excluded from every backup. Text is included only in your iPhone’s standard iCloud backup if enabled. That backup is stored in your own iCloud.
