Privacy in detail

AI note taker that does not collect your data

NoteCord is an app for iPhone and iPad that records, transcribes and summarizes conversations while keeping recordings and text on your device. NoteCord is an AI note-taking app with the App Store privacy label “Data Not Collected”; summaries with action points require Apple Intelligence.

Data Not Collected

NoteCord’s App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. NoteCord requires no account. NoteCord never sends your recordings to us. They only reach someone else if you share them. There is no NoteCord server.

Your conversations stay on your iPhone.

NoteCord records and transcribes on your device. It also creates summaries on your device. Summaries with action points require Apple Intelligence.

Audio stays out of backups.

Audio stays on your device and is excluded from every backup. Text stays on your device. It is included only in your iPhone’s regular iCloud backup if enabled, in your own iCloud.

Remember a word. Find the conversation.

Search across all your conversations on your device. Organize them in folders by project or client. You do not need a NoteCord account.

Questions people ask

Is there an AI note-taking app with a Data Not Collected privacy label?
Yes. NoteCord’s App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.
Does NoteCord send my recordings to you or anyone else?
No. NoteCord never sends your recordings to us. They only reach someone else if you share them. There is no NoteCord server.
Do I need an account to use NoteCord?
No. NoteCord records and transcribes on your device without an account.
Are my recordings included in any backup?
No. Audio stays on your device and is excluded from every backup. Text is included only in your iPhone’s regular iCloud backup if enabled, in your own iCloud.
Can NoteCord see my conversations?
No. There is no NoteCord server and you do not need an account. We cannot see your recordings or text.
