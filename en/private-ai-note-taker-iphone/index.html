What goes into a backup?

Audio is never included in any backup. Text goes into your own iCloud only through your iPhone’s standard iCloud backup, if that backup is enabled. Whether to use that backup stays your choice.

Questions people ask

Does NoteCord upload my recordings to the cloud?
No. NoteCord never sends your recordings to us. They only reach someone else if you share them. There is no NoteCord server.
Does NoteCord collect my data?
No. “Data Not Collected” is Apple’s App Store privacy label. We have no server of our own and cannot see your recordings or text.
Do I need an account?
No. You can use NoteCord without an account.
Does my text go into iCloud?
Yes, but only through your iPhone’s standard iCloud backup, if it is enabled. Your text is then included in that backup in your own iCloud. Audio is never included in any backup.
Does NoteCord transcribe on my iPhone or on a server?
NoteCord transcribes on your iPhone. There is no NoteCord server.
Can I record a lecture or meeting with my screen locked?
Yes. Recording continues with your screen locked. You can see it in Dynamic Island and on the lock screen, and mark a moment from the lock screen.
